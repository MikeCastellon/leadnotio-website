Features

Everything you need to close deals

A full outreach CRM built directly into your Shopify admin — no separate tools, no tab switching.

📋

Pipeline Status Tracking

Assign statuses like New, Contacted, Qualified, Closed Won, or Closed Lost to every customer and keep your pipeline organized at a glance.

📝

Notes & Activity Log

Log calls, emails, and meetings directly on customer profiles. Your entire communication history lives in one place.

📅

Follow-Up Reminders

Set next-contact dates on any customer so your team always knows who to reach out to and when — nothing slips through the cracks.

👥

Team Assignments

Assign customers to specific team members. Everyone knows their accounts, and managers can see the full team pipeline in one view.

🔍

Saved Views

Filter by status, owner, tags, follow-up date, or order history — then save your filters as one-click views for your whole team.

💌

Email Templates

Save your best outreach messages as reusable templates. Send consistent, personalized emails faster without rewriting from scratch.

➕

Add Customers Manually

Create new leads directly inside LeadNotio. Sync them to Shopify or keep them local — your choice based on your workflow.

📊

Outreach Analytics

See pipeline health at a glance — how many customers are in each stage, team performance, and where deals are getting stuck.

🏪

100% Inside Shopify

No external dashboards, no separate logins. LeadNotio lives right inside your Shopify admin — where your team already works every day.
